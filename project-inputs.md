# Mozambik Somerset West — UGC Advertising Project

## Business

**Business Name:** Mozambik – Somerset West

**Business Type:** Afro-Porto casual dining restaurant

**Location:** The Sanctuary Shopping Centre, Somerset West, South Africa

---

## Target Audience

Families, couples, friends and local groups looking for a relaxed, social and flavourful dining experience.

---

## Customer Problem

Customers may become bored with visiting the same restaurants and may struggle to choose a restaurant that offers something enjoyable for everyone in their group.

---

## Product / Service

Mozambik's restaurant dining experience, including:

* Famous chicken
* Peri-peri chicken
* Garlic-lemon-herb chicken
* Prawns
* Portuguese-inspired dishes
* Vegetarian options
* Vegan options

---

## Unique Value

Mozambik combines African and Portuguese influences with signature sauces, famous chicken and a relaxed beach-bar atmosphere.

---

## Main Benefit

A flavourful and social dining experience that feels like a mini escape without leaving Somerset West.

---

## Tone

Authentic, conversational, friendly, relaxed and slightly playful.

The content should feel like a genuine recommendation rather than a traditional advertisement.

---

## Primary Platform

Instagram Reels

## Secondary Platforms

YouTube Shorts

Paid Social Ads

---

## Primary CTA

Book a table.

---

## Secondary CTAs

* Visit the restaurant
* Save the restaurant
* Send the video to friends
* View the menu
* Plan the next dinner

---

## Campaign Objective

Generate awareness and encourage local customers to visit or book a table at Mozambik Somerset West.
